Project A: SQL Sales Analysis

In this project, I designed a relational database schema for a Hospital Management System using PostgreSQL.

I focused on linking patient demographics with clinical visit records to streamline administrative tasks and improve patient care through proactive data monitoring.

Simply put, the goal is to reorganize patient information within different sheets, find patients and their contacts whom has yet to make the next appointment.

Lastly, prioritize patients that have their temperature abnormally high.

First, I created a table that would bring the patients data sheet.
